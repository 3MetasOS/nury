Nury

The crisis-response agent for solo pastors.

Juan Pelaez · 3Metas · Gloo AI Hackathon · Track 1

The user

  • A solo pastor. No staff. No lawyer on the line.
  • A panicked call from an immigrant family. Sunday afternoon, or 2 AM.
  • They have a phone and minutes. Not a plan.

Humanitarian, not political. A family and a night.

Playbook #1: detention. Five stages, the pastor decides each.

1 Triage

Facts only. No advice.

2 Rights brief

Family's language. Vetted source. Cited.

3 Attorneys

Hotlines and church-vetted entries. Never one named attorney.

4 Checklist

Do tonight. Do not do. Gather documents.

5 Message

Under 120 words. Warm and steady.

Demo: Maria, Jose, two children (8 and 11). Live app or the 90-second video.

Safety you can see

Approve · Edit · Stop after every stage.

Draft rejected by guardrail → Regenerating (2 of 3) → Passed

  • Up to three tries, then escalate to the pastor.
  • The pastor never sees the unsafe draft.
  • No send path. Copy, not Send.

How it is built

  • One engine runs crisis playbooks. Detention is playbook #1.
  • Adding a crisis is adding a playbook folder. The engine does not change. Tested with a test playbook.
  • Gloo guarded Responses endpoint.
  • Legal information only, from a vetted source file. No open web.
  • Not a pastor, counselor, therapist or lawyer. Says so on every output.
  • English screen for the pastor. Spanish output for the family.
