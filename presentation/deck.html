Innovation: a crisis is a folder, and drafts correct themselves

One engine, many playbooks

Adding a crisis is adding a playbook folder. The engine does not change. We tested that with a test playbook.

Playbooks share small skills, like voice and grounding. A stage includes them by name.

Unsafe drafts never reach the pastor

Every draft is checked. An unsafe draft is rejected and regenerated, up to three tries, then escalated to the pastor.

The pastor sees the accepted draft only.

1:50 to 2:15. Criterion: Innovation. Two ideas: the playbook folder (confirmed, tested on a test playbook; do not claim a second crisis is scored unless Sensei confirms) and the self-correction loop with a human gate. Do not mention skills or the church network unless they are switched on.

Privacy that is tested

  • Names, phones, emails, addresses, dates of birth and IDs become tokens before a request leaves the computer. The map stays local.
  • A leak test captures the exact request body at the HTTP boundary. A synthetic intake full of canary names and numbers goes through all five stages.
  • Limits, said plainly: direct identifiers only. Context can still hint. A name the pastor did not protect is not removed.
  • Cost: one pastoral message lost the family's name.
