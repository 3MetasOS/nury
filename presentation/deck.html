Nury

The crisis-response agent for solo pastors.

Juan Pelaez · 3Metas · Gloo AI Hackathon · Track 1

2:07 AM. A solo pastor's phone rings.

  • A solo pastor. No staff. No lawyer on the line.
  • A panicked call from an immigrant family. Sunday afternoon, or 2 AM.
  • They have a phone and a few minutes.

Nury is humanitarian and takes no political side. The subject is a family and a night.

The app opens on a crisis selector

[SCREENSHOT SLOT: crisis selector from the live app, phone width. Capture after hack-artisans ships it.]
  • Detention flagship, the demo
  • Hospital emergency [confirm live in writing before showing]
  • Sudden death in a family coming soon
  • House fire or displacement coming soon

Each card is a playbook. Nothing is claimed about the coming-soon cards.

Playbook #1: detention. Five stages, the pastor decides each.

  1. TriageStates the facts without advice.
  2. Rights briefFamily's language. Vetted source. Every point cited.
  3. Attorney resourcesHotlines and church-vetted entries. Never one named attorney.
  4. Family checklistDo tonight. Do not do. Gather these documents.
  5. Pastoral messageUnder 120 words. Warm and steady.

Demo: Maria, Jose, two children (8 and 11). Live app or the 90-second video.

Safety you can see

Approve · Edit · Stop after every stage.

Draft rejected by guardrail → Regenerating (2 of 3) → Passed

  • Up to three tries, then escalate to the pastor.
  • The pastor never sees the unsafe draft.
  • Nury has no send path. The pastor copies the text and sends it by hand.

How it is built

  • One engine runs crisis playbooks. Detention is playbook #1.
  • Adding a crisis is adding a playbook folder. The engine does not change. Tested with a test playbook.
  • Gloo guarded Responses endpoint.
  • Legal information only, from a vetted source file. No open web.
  • Nury is not a pastor, counselor, therapist or lawyer, and says so on every output.
  • English screen for the pastor. Spanish output for the family.
