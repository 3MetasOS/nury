Nury. An AI Crisis Response Agent.

Nury is a crisis-management engine that runs playbooks. Its first playbook is a family detention.

It is 2 AM. A pastor's phone rings. A panicked immigrant family says a relative was detained. There is no lawyer on the line, only a phone.

Nury turns that call into a complete response package. The pastor types what the family said. Nury runs five stages: triage the case, write a plain-language rights brief, list attorney resources, build a family checklist, and draft a short pastoral message. Family materials come out in Spanish.

The pastor stays in charge. After every stage, the pastor can Approve, Edit, or Stop. Later stages use the pastor's edits. Nury has no send path. Nothing reaches the family except through the pastor.

Nury gives legal information only, never advice or predictions. Every rights point cites a vetted source file. There is no open web. Every output carries a disclaimer and urges an attorney. Nury never recommends a specific attorney. Nury is not a pastor, counselor, or lawyer.

Each draft passes named rule checks and a Jev classifier. An unsafe draft is rejected and regenerated, up to three tries, then escalated. The pastor never sees it.
