# Presenter notes: what to say that the slides do NOT say (2:45)

The slides carry the words. You carry the reason and the proof. Do not read a slide aloud. [Space] = key press. Every number is from the audit table.

## 1 · The call (0:00 to 0:22)
Slide: the time, the husband, "heart and a phone".
Say: "When a family is in crisis, the first call often goes to their pastor. Not a lawyer. At 2 AM that pastor is searching a phone and hoping the answer is right. In an immigration case, a wrong answer can do real harm."

## 2 · The name (0:22 to 0:38)
Slide: So we built Nury, the entry, "never alone".
Say: "Nur is light. Nury is an AI agent that works beside the pastor. It does the heavy preparation, so the pastor can do what only a pastor can do."

## 3 · What changes (0:38 to 1:02)
Slide: guide, cited answers, first draft, "the pastor leads".
Say: "Every crisis runs the same five careful stages. The rights brief comes only from vetted sources, every point cited. It's legal information, never advice. Names are hidden before the model sees them. A first draft in under a minute, in Spanish."

## 4 · The demo (1:02 to 2:05)
Slide: the real app, sped up 3x, no sound.
[Space, Space] Start the film.
Say, early: "This is the real app, sped up three times."
Say, at the gates: "After each stage the pastor approves, edits, or stops."
Say, on the Spanish page: "The Scripture is never written by the model. It's chosen from an approved list and quoted word for word."
Last 8 seconds: say nothing.

## 5 · Beside the pastor (2:05 to 2:35)
Slide: writes and tracks, built to learn, nothing sent, "works for the pastor".
Say: "Nury writes the documents, saves every case, and logs every check with its reasons. We tested 46 made-up cases: 29 passed, 2 failed, 15 undecided. No pastor has used it yet. It's built to learn, with the pastor's approval."

## 6 · Close (2:35 to 2:45)
Say: "The next call will come. Nury will be there when the pastor picks up." Then stop. Let the two small lines sit.

About 205 spoken words: 85 seconds of speech. With the film, presses and pauses, a run is about 2:45.

## If you run long, cut
The Scripture line. Then the tested-cases line.

## If a judge asks
- Who built the checks? Our 20 named rules, plus Jev, a decision API from TypeSafe. We use it, we did not build it.
- Is it live? Two crises are. The film is a recorded run of the real app; the checks ran for real.
- Does it send anything? No. Nothing leaves except by the pastor.
- What does it cost? 6 to 9 cents a case, 34 to 50 seconds of model time.

## Do not say
"ICE" or any agency. "Legal advice". "Confirmed". That a pastor, an attorney or a family has used Nury.
