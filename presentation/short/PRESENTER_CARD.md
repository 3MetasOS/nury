## Say, if asked
- It gives legal information, never advice. An attorney is the next step.
- "Cited answers" means the rights brief comes only from vetted sources, each point cited.
- "A first draft in under a minute" is the model time (34 to 50 seconds). The pastor reads and approves after.
- "Built to learn": new crises are new playbooks. The learning loop is built, off by default, and applies nothing without approval.
- Jev is a decision API from TypeSafe. We use it. We did not build it.
- 46 made-up cases: 29 pass, 2 fail, 15 undecided. No real pastor has used it yet.

## Never say
- "ICE" or any agency. "Legal advice" (say "legal information"). "Confirmed".
- That a pastor, an attorney or a family has used Nury.
- Her name aloud before the close.
