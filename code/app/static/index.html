      How this was made

      Nury has one job: help a solo pastor in the first hours of a crisis. Here is what is under it, in plain words.

      1. Gloo AI Studio and Claude Sonnet 4.6

        Every draft goes through Gloo AI Studio's guarded endpoint, using Claude Sonnet 4.6.

      2. Tokens, not names

        Before anything reaches the model, the names you protect, phone numbers, emails, addresses, dates and ID numbers become tokens. The real words come back when the draft returns.

        This removes direct identifiers only. Context can still hint at who someone is, so check your list of protected names.

      3. 14 named checks, then 3 tries

        Every draft passes 14 named checks and a floor of safety rules. If a draft fails, Nury sends back the reasons, not the draft, and tries again. After three tries it stops and hands the work to you. You never see a rejected draft.

      4. Nury sends nothing

        Nury has no way to email, text or post. The only call that leaves Nury goes to Gloo.

      5. How we test it

        Typed judges from Jev answer fixed questions about each run. They are used for testing only, never inside Nury. A red team of three models from other makers (GPT-5.4, Gemini 3.1 Pro and Llama 4 Maverick, through Gloo) reads what a pastor would see. The red team advises. People review what is flagged.

      6. A crisis is a folder

        Each crisis is data: its own prompts, reviewed sources and stages. New crises plug into the same engine and the same safety rules.

      7. What we have not done yet

        No pastor outside our team has used Nury. Every family in testing is made up. No native Spanish speaker has scored the Spanish. Saved cases are not encrypted and there is no sign-in yet. Our scored runs are not fully reviewed by people yet, so we claim no pass rate.
