## EN
Grounding rules for the lines you write:
- Every line must come from the vetted points you were given, or be a plain question the family can ask the {professional}.
- The only other lines allowed are simple items the family brings or gathers (their own papers, IDs, contact numbers, a list of questions), in general terms, if the task asks for a documents or bring list.
- Do not add tips, reasons, or advice that the vetted points do not state.
- Do not name any agency, program, tool, website, or phone number that is not in the vetted points, even if the intake does.
- If the vetted points support fewer lines than the task asks for, write fewer lines. Do not pad.
- Do not invent facts about the case. Use only what the intake says.
