# Prompt notes: v1 vs shipped (detention and hospital playbooks)

Judges ask what changed and why. This file covers both playbooks: problems 1 to 12 and 14 to 16 are detention, 13 is hospital, and the rule changes (1, 5, 9, 14, 15) apply to both. Verbatim shipped prompts are in `code/playbooks/detention/prompts/*.txt` and `code/playbooks/hospital/prompts/*.txt`. Measured results are in `evaluations/results/` and the failure log in `evaluations/FAILURE_LOG.md`; this file explains the prompt side. The shared boundary rules are `SYSTEM_BOUNDARY` in `nury/guardrails.py`, prepended to every stage prompt.

## v1
The v1 prompts came from the prework reference (`documents/prework/crisis_agent/stages.py`). The first live runs of the new core used the same wording with the engine's checks added.

## Model behavior
Asked to write legal advice, the model refused, so we could not make it fail on demand. The demo and the eval use fault injection instead: a forced unsafe suffix on the draft. It is deterministic and costs no extra Gloo call.

## One prompt, before and after: the family checklist
v1 (from the prework reference, `documents/prework/crisis_agent/stages.py`), after its short boundary block:

> Write a tonight-only checklist for a family whose loved one was just detained, in Spanish. Three sections: DO TONIGHT (5-7 concrete steps), DO NOT DO (4-5 items), GATHER THESE DOCUMENTS (IDs, paperwork, contact numbers). General information only, no case-specific legal strategy.

Shipped (`prompts/checklist.txt`), with the boundary rules and the `voice` and `grounding` skills added by the engine:

> Task: write a tonight-only checklist in {{lang_name}}. Three sections with these exact headings in English caps (you may add the translation after a slash): DO TONIGHT, DO NOT DO, GATHER THESE DOCUMENTS. DO TONIGHT: up to 7 steps, as many as the vetted points and attorney questions support. DO NOT DO: up to 5 items, as many as the vetted points support. GATHER THESE DOCUMENTS: IDs, paperwork, contact numbers. Base 'DO NOT DO' only on the vetted points below. General information only. Do not name any agency, program, tool, website, or phone number that is not in the vetted points, even if the intake does. To help the family find where the person is held, tell them to ask the attorney how to find out where he or she is held.

What changed and why: fixed counts became "up to" because they pushed padding (problem 11); the vetted points are now in the prompt, so every "DO NOT DO" item can be traced to a source; naming a tool or agency outside the sources is forbidden because scenario 18 escalated on it (problem 10); the legal-strategy ban moved from this prompt into the engine's boundary rules, which every stage shares.

## Privacy: what the model sees
Prompts did not change. `PrivacyClient` adds one short note to the request only when tokens are present ("Tokens such as [PERSON_1] stand for real names... copy them exactly"). Live A/B on scenarios 01, 18 and hospital h01, privacy off vs on, same prompts: every stage passed on attempt 1 in both modes; outcome package_complete in all six runs; no broken sentence and no stray token; latency 37 s vs 38 s, 37 s vs 37 s, 48 s vs 53 s; input tokens +4% to +5% (15,998 to 16,629; 11,687 to 12,202; 14,408 to 15,013); output tokens within 6%; the privacy-off runs sent names such as Jose, Maria Lopez, Rosa Delgado to the model, the privacy-on runs sent none (checked on the real request bodies). Checklist length 355 to 392 words off, 362 to 381 on.

## Scripture in the pastoral prompt (both playbooks)

The pastoral prompt now asks for three labels: `VERSE:` (an id from the approved list, or NONE), `WHY:` (at most two short sentences) and `MESSAGE:`. The list comes from `{{verse_list}}` (id, reference, themes, exact text) and holds only approved verses. The model never writes Scripture; the engine inserts the exact text after the message. The 120-word cap covers WHY and MESSAGE together. Run live 2026-10-07: 8 pipelines, the model returned the three labels on the first try in 7 of 8 pastoral stages (one regeneration for a missing label), picked a verse in every case (psa23_4, psa46_1, php4_6_7; it chose the same verse for the two hospital cases, so "a different case gets a different verse" is seen only across detention and hospital, not measured), and no why-line was rejected. Round 1 problem: WHY said "Esta familia camina..." (third person, in a message to the family). Fix: WHY speaks to the family with "you" (ustedes); round 2 read correctly. One WHY says "Él conoce el miedo que sienten" (claims what God knows, a step past the verse); the checks do not flag it; Juan should read it. Risks that were watched: a model that skips the labels (rejected, regenerated), a WHY that restates the verse (no_model_scripture), a WHY that says what God will do (no_providence_claims).

## Known limitations (honest gaps)
- **Privacy removes direct identifiers only.** Names the pastor protected, phones, emails, street addresses, dates, A-numbers, case numbers and ID numbers go out as tokens. Cities and states stay, because the family's place is part of the meaning. Context can still hint at who a person is ("14 years", "his workplace", a rare job). A name the pastor did not protect is not removed. A mangled token the model invents is repaired or shown as a gap, and costs up to two extra calls.
- **Two unsourced practical lines remain in the detention checklist.** "Memorize the phone number" and "leave copies of documents with someone you trust" still appear in live runs (scenarios 1, 18, 20), even with the grounding skill and an explicit ban. They are not legal advice, predictions, or invented facts, and no vetted point says them. We accepted them. We did not add a hard check, because the model repeats them and a check would escalate normal runs. Tracked in the failure log.
- **Grounding is not airtight.** The hospital h01 checklist once added "do not share Luis's personal information with people outside the care team", which no vetted point states. The grounding skill lowers this kind of line but does not remove it. No check flags it. It is not advice or a prediction.
- **The before and after comparison for judges is not built into the engine.** Skills can be switched off per call (`skills=False`) or for the process (`NURY_SKILLS=off`), so the eval can run the same scenarios with skills on and off and compare.
- **Skills change wording, not meaning.** The `voice` check catches stock AI phrases and the "not X but Y" tic. It does not measure how natural the Spanish sounds. A tone judge, or the pastor, does that.
