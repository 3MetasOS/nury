# Judging process — Gloo AI Hackathon

Sources: official rules doc (read 2026-10-05), Gloo FAQ, Discord organizer post
(2026-10-06).

## Preliminary judging — Oct 7

- Submission deadline: working solution + 250-word English description by
  **9:00 PM MDT** (video optional at this stage).
- Judging window: **9:00–11:00 PM MDT** (per FAQ).
- Format (Discord organizer post, 2026-10-06): teams get **3 minutes** to convince
  judges — video, live presentation, or a combination, team's choice.
- **Conflict to verify at the event:** the rules doc says "every entrant gives a
  90-second summary"; the Discord post says 3 minutes. The Discord post is newer.
  Also, the post closes with "excited for next week," which suggests it may be a
  repost — confirm the 3-minute format is current for 2026.
- **Top 25** announced at **midnight** → finals.

## Finals — Oct 8

- Finalists submit a **90-second demo video by 9:00 AM MDT**.
- Finalists present during the closing session (live presentation length TBD —
  ask at the event).
- Tight turnaround: midnight announcement → 9 AM video deadline. If we make top
  25, the video gets built overnight.

## Scoring (20% each)

1. Concept / Product
2. Innovation
3. Impact and Execution — **first tie-break**
4. Use of AI
5. Teamwork / Presentation

## Deliverables this creates

1. **3-minute prelim pitch** (Oct 7 evening) — video and/or live. NEW vs the
   earlier 90-second assumption; the deck script needs a 3-minute version.
2. **250-word description** — Oct 7, 9:00 PM MDT.
3. **90-second finalist video** — Oct 8, 9:00 AM MDT, only if top 25.
