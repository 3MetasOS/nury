  "sadly won't be able to make the hackathon anymore" but wanted to share the
  project. Not competing at the event.
- **Site:** https://getrockpool.com/
- **Status:** In development, waitlist only. "Might launch it publicly soon."

## What it is

"AI that finds the work worth automating." Targets small-church and church-plant
pastors who don't have time to figure out the highest-leverage ways to deploy
agents.

How it works (per site):
1. Connect your apps (email, calendar, docs — permissions explained up front).
2. Rockpool analyzes work data and finds repeated tasks across those apps.
3. It recommends workflows; the pastor reviews and chooses what gets automated.
4. Rockpool deploys and manages the agents.

Example discoveries shown: weekly attendance report prep, comparing attendance
across Sundays, Sunday service planning, newcomer follow-up, elders' monthly update.

## Claimed innovation

The discovery system: finding repeated work that is both appropriate to automate
AND high-leverage for the pastor — not just a chatbot, but an automation scout.

## Assessment vs our entry

- **Different problem.** Rockpool = routine, repeated ops work ("what should I
  automate?"). Ours = acute crisis response ("help me handle this right now").
- **Different automation philosophy.** Rockpool hands work off to agents; we keep
  the pastor approving every stage because crisis moments must not be fully
  automated. These are complementary, not overlapping.
- **Threat level:** Low for the hackathon (not attending). Medium as market
  landscape — waitlist with referral mechanics suggests a real launch attempt.
- **If judges ask:** "Rockpool automates the routine so pastors have margin;
  we're built for the 2 AM crisis when margin runs out — structured workflow,
  guardrail correction loops, pastor approval at every step."
