## What it is

An application for pastors, missionaries, and ministry leaders. Flow:

1. User describes their day, responsibilities, and "what they are carrying."
2. AI analyzes the situation: workload, pace, recovery, friction.
3. Insights turn into decisions: rest, delegate, prioritize, simplify.
4. Priorities become tasks via an Eisenhower Matrix, organized in a ministry calendar.

- **Tagline:** "Sustained to Serve."
- **Wedge:** pastor sustainability / burnout prevention — caring for the caregiver.
