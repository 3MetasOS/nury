## Our differentiation (all entries)

- We do **crisis response**, not routine automation: high-stakes, non-routine,
  2 AM moments where full automation is the wrong answer.
- **Pastor-operated workflow** with approval gates after every stage — the pastor
  stays the minister; the agent is the response team.
- **Iterative guardrail correction**: unsafe drafts are rejected and regenerated
  (max 3 tries) before the pastor ever sees them; self-corrections are logged.
- **Vetted sources only**, no open-web browsing for legal-adjacent facts.
- Complementary framing if asked: "Rockpool automates the routine so pastors
  have margin; we show up for the crisis when margin runs out."
