# Who Gloo's customers are (read 2026-10-07, search results, pages not all opened)

- Gloo (Boulder, Colorado; founded 2013) describes more than **140,000 churches, ministry and non-profit leaders** using its services, and more than **70,000 churches** using Gloo to connect. [Christian Post, "With thousands of churches as clients, Gloo seeks to raise $100M in IPO"](https://assets.christianpost.com/news/gloo-seeks-to-raise-100m-in-ipo-to-advance-human-flourishing.html); [Gloo, Our Aspiration](https://gloo.us/about-us?hsLang=en)
- Clients and partners named in coverage: church **denominations and networks**, recovery centers and parachurch ministries, including **Compassion International, Cru, FamilyLife, Alpha, Urban Ministries International and MOPS**; contracts with **Wycliffe Bible Translators** and the **American Bible Society**. The ecosystem includes ministries, service organizations, educational institutions, health providers, nonprofits and values-aligned organizations. (same Christian Post article; [Capterra listing](https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1013115/Gloo))
- The 2026 hackathon (Oct 6 to 8, Boulder) targets "Applied AI for the faith ecosystem": tools for nonprofits, churches, ministries, Bible organizations, universities and mission-driven communities; three tracks (Agents powered by Gloo, Ministry Resourcing powered by Masterworks, Bible powered by YouVersion); about 700 builders; Gloo AI Studio for governed, values-aligned AI. [Gloo press release](https://gloo.com/press/releases/gloo-announces-2026-ai-hackathon); [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260528477852/en/)
- The press release states $250,000 in cash prizes; the official rules document we read earlier says $200,000. Use the rules document.
