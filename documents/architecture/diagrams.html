System context

The pastor talks to the Nury app on a phone. Nury calls one outside service, the Gloo guarded Responses endpoint, and reads only the vetted source files. Every call, check, and decision goes to the audit log. Nury has no web access and no way to send anything to the family. The pastor shares text by hand.

Where it lives
  • code/app/server.py
  • code/nury/gloo_client.py
  • platform.ai.gloo.com
  • POST /ai/v2/guarded/responses
  • code/nury/audit.py

Layers and ownership

The app and the eval harness both call the engine. The engine owns running a stage, the gates, chaining, and the audit log. The safety floor lives inside the engine, in code, not in data. A playbook is data. It can add checks and words. The loader refuses a playbook that drops the floor wording.

Where it lives
  • code/app
  • code/nury/engine.py, guardrails.py, checks.py, playbook.py
  • code/playbooks/<id>/
  • evaluations/

Anatomy of a playbook

A new crisis is a new folder. The loader checks the folder before the engine uses it. Paths are plain field matches on the triage text, so a stage can run, be skipped, or swap its prompt. Detention is the first playbook. The hospital playbook is a test fixture that proves the engine needs no change. A real second playbook is planned for later.

Where it lives
  • code/playbooks/detention/
  • code/nury/playbook.py
  • code/tests/test_core.py

One run, five stages

Each stage ends at a gate: Approve, Edit, or Stop. Later stages read the approved or edited text, so a pastor's edit carries forward. A run ends in one of four outcomes. Nothing is sent. The pastor shares the package.

Where it lives
  • code/nury/engine.py run_pipeline
  • code/playbooks/detention/stages.json
  • code/playbooks/detention/outcomes.json

Correction loop, one stage

The engine drafts, checks, and either shows a safe draft or rejects it with a reason category. A rejected draft goes to the audit log and the eval record, never to the pastor. The engine regenerates with the reasons, not the draft. Three attempts in total, then it escalates and shows no draft. The fault-injection point is how the demo and the evals force a rejection.

Where it lives
  • code/nury/engine.py run_stage
  • code/nury/guardrails.py
  • code/nury/checks.py

Eval harness

Each scenario runs through an adapter that drives the real engine with scripted gate decisions and optional fault injection. Deterministic judges score the trajectory with no AI. Jev judges add typed verdicts at eval time only. The scorecard collects both. GLOO_API_KEY is used at runtime. JEV_API_KEY is used only by the eval judges.

Where it lives
  • evaluations/adapter_nury.py
  • evaluations/judges/
  • evaluations/run.py
  • evaluations/results/
