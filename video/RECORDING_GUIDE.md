# Recording guide for Juan (one page)

**What you need:** your phone, a steady place to put it, a window, a quiet room. Nothing else.

1. **Phone.** Record in 1080p (Settings, Camera, Video, 1080p at 30 fps). Landscape is best for the film. Vertical works too: we put it in a soft frame.
2. **Place it.** Put the phone on something steady (books, a shelf) at eye height, about an arm and a half away. Do not hold it. Lock focus by tapping your face.
3. **Light.** Face a window. The light should be in front of you, not behind. No lamp behind your head. Turn off a ceiling light that makes shadows under your eyes.
4. **Sound.** Quiet room. Close the window if there is traffic. Turn off fans, the fridge hum if you can, notifications and music. Do not play any music: we add our own.
5. **Say it slowly.** Slower than feels natural. Short sentences. Breathe between them. Look at the lens, not at the screen.
6. **Three takes.** Record the whole thing three times. You pick the best one. Do not worry about mistakes: keep going, or start the sentence again.
7. **Silence at both ends.** Start the recording, count five slow seconds, then speak. When you finish, stay still and count five more before you stop.
8. **Send it.** AirDrop or email the original file (not a screenshot, not a chat app copy). Put it in `video/incoming/`.

**What happens next:** we clean the sound (hiss, hum, loudness), cut it to fit, put it in a soft frame on the paper look and add captions from the words you give us. About 15 minutes after the file arrives.

**What we cannot do:** we cannot hear or judge how you sound. You choose the take. We do not change your words or speed you up more than 5 percent.
**Where it could go:** the memorial (your own words), or a 5-second closing line. Say which.
