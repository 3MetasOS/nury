# Read sheet: the 10 voiceover lines (locked text)

Read each line on its own, calm and warm, like talking to one person at 2 AM. Leave half a second of quiet before and after each line. Do not rush. One take per line is fine; do two for the long ones.
Record in a quiet room, phone 20 cm from your mouth, a little off to the side. Save one file per line: `01.wav` ... `10.wav` (any format is fine, we clean it).
Clean with `video/vo/clean_voice.sh 01_raw.m4a 01.wav`, then put the files in `video/remotion/public/vo/`.
